Feedstock

Transforming agricultural waste
into high-value biochar.

Our technology handles a diverse range of agricultural residues. Whether your waste is fibrous (stems), porous (cobs), dense (shells) or mineral-heavy (leafy waste), we optimise the conversion so the resulting biochar solves your specific soil problem.

Fibrous

Stems and stalks

High-volume, low-density residue that burns fast in the open and is awkward to store. Good structure for a porous char.

Porous

Cobs and husks

Corn cob, rice husk and patchouli waste — consistent, abundant, and already aggregated at the mill or drying floor.

Dense

Shells and wood

Slow to decompose and high in fixed carbon. Produces the most durable char per tonne of feedstock.

Mineral-heavy

Leafy waste

Lower carbon yield, higher nutrient return — useful where the soil benefit matters as much as the tonne.
