Carbon removal solutions
tailored for everyone.

Select your role to see how we turn agricultural waste into climate value. The process is the same one either way — what changes is which end of it you are standing at.

For millers and processors

Still treating agricultural waste as a disposal burden?

By integrating small-scale pyrolysis, we help regional millers convert agricultural waste into measurable carbon removal. Supporting a circular economy has never been this accessible.

  • Disposal cost removed
  • Revenue share on issued credits
  • On-site unit, operated by us

For farms, plantations, fertiliser and AgTech companies

Looking to enhance soil performance while advancing sustainable agriculture?

High-quality biochar supports healthier soils, improved yields and long-term resilience for your operations — while the carbon underneath is what the market is buying.

  • Higher water retention
  • Yield and drought resilience
  • Biochar bulk purchase

For companies pursuing net-zero targets

Exploring credible pathways toward net zero?

Biochar offers measurable carbon removal through insetting and verified carbon credit programmes — engineered, century-durable, and traceable to a named project.

  • Batch-level traceability
  • Forward offtake on named projects
  • Aligned to ICVCM and SBTi

For global supply chain leaders

Ready to turn global commodity flows into climate assets?

Embed biochar technology at the source to meet Scope 3 targets and lock carbon away for centuries. Converting vast residue streams into climate-smart commodities delivers value to downstream buyers and local farmers alike.

  • Insetting within existing suppliers
  • Supplier-level impact reporting
  • Scope 3 contribution

For waste management companies & local governments

Seeking smarter ways to convert organic waste into long-term environmental value?

Biochar systems transform waste streams into soil-enhancing resources while reducing emissions — and produce the paperwork that proves where the tonnage went.

  • Methane and NO₂ avoided
  • Audited diversion records
  • Landfill pressure reduced
