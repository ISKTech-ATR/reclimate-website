About us

Finding inspiration
in every turn.

Reclimate began with a mission to make climate action accessible to everyone, starting with farming communities. What started as one site in Malaysia turning agricultural waste into biochar has grown into a broader mission across the Global South.

Today Reclimate collaborates with farmers across the region, combining practical field knowledge with our own digital platform to give farmers, enterprises and partners fair access to global carbon markets and climate solutions.

What we stand for

Four things we
will not trade away.

01

Science with integrity

Let’s be real — the carbon world can be messy. People overclaim, underprove, and hope no one notices. We build trust through transparency and continuous validation. From soil carbon measurements to digital MRV, our removals are verified, traceable and legit. We bring data receipts for everything.

02

Empowerment through inclusion

We are flipping the table: farmers are not the underdogs, they are the real heroes of this movement. We don’t want smallholders to merely survive climate change — we want them to profit from fighting it, with the tools, knowledge and access to participate meaningfully.

03

Technology for equity

Our platform isn’t a confusing, high-tech maze. It’s built for people who actually work the land — easy, transparent and fair. We remove the barriers of cost, complexity and scale so farmers can track performance, quantify impact and reach carbon markets with confidence.

04

Turning waste into worth

Piles of waste sitting there, waiting to rot. What most people see as trash, we see as treasure. We turn that waste into biochar, soil boosters and real carbon storage — taking what everyone ignores and making it the most valuable thing in the room.
